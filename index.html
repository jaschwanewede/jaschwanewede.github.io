About Me

Get to know me!

Hello and welcome to my page! I'm Jack Schwanewede, from Ridgewood NJ, and I graduated from Davidson College this past May.

My research and experience has given me the opportunity to work on projects that combine computer science, psychology, and data science. I've built virtual reality experiments, analyzed behavioral data, and developed machine learning models. I enjoy figuring out how different pieces fit together to answer interesting questions and have real impact.

Outside of my work, I like staying involved and investing time into my hobbies. At Davidson, I was the principal double bassist in the Symphony Orchestra, played bass guitar in several student bands, and served as Vice President and Social Chair of the Davidson Games Club. One of the highlights was helping organize an event where I interviewed MatPat in front of more than 400 attendees. I'm also a big sports fan and stay active through pickup basketball and Spikeball with friends.

Research Experience

VR Researcher — Davidson Research in Immersive Virtual Environments

Programmed virtual and mixed reality experiments in Unity and C#, analyzed data in R, first author on two publications in IEEE TVCG and IEEE VR.
Adviser: Tabitha Peck, PhD

Data Engineer and Research Adviser — Davidson VGSocLab

Scraped and analyzed data on video game characters using Python and Beautiful Soup, advised research directives, presented work at Southern Sociological Society.
Adviser: Jeremy Brenner-Levoy, PhD

VR Research Assistant — University College London, Solomon Lab

Redesigned VR experiments on top-down perception and agency using Unity; collaborated with researchers to refine and optimize experimental design.
Adviser: Sam Solomon, PhD

Projects

Field Hockey Corner Analyzer

Built an HTML/JavaScript web app to track and analyze field hockey corner plays for Davidson College Athletics. Used for scouting and strategic play-calling improvements.

Field Hockey Project

NBA 2K Rating and All-Star Predictive Model

Developed a multi-task deep learning model using PyTorch to predict NBA 2K player ratings and season awards. Trained on NBA season data and presented methodology in a technical report.

NBA 2K Model

Video Game Network Graph

Created an interactive d3.js visualization mapping crossovers between characters in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, allowing users to explore network relationships between franchises.
